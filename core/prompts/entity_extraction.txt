1. NAMED ENTITIES: Organizations, people, geographic/political entities, laws, products, and events mentioned in the contract. For each, note whether it is at risk of changing identity (e.g. a company that could be acquired, a candidate who could drop out).

2. NUMERIC THRESHOLDS: Any numeric value that the contract's resolution depends on. For each, identify the data series it references, whether that series is known to be revised after initial release, and whether the contract depends on a single data point.

3. TIMEFRAMES: All date references, period references ("Q3", "by year end", "within 30 days"), and deadline language. Note whether timezone is specified and whether there are fiscal vs calendar year ambiguities.

4. KEY TERMS: Words or phrases that carry legal or definitional weight but are not defined in the contract. Flag terms that have multiple common interpretations.

5. SOURCE URL: Extract any URL from the resolution source field. If the resolution source is plain text (not a URL), set source_url to null.

Return ONLY a valid JSON object. No preamble, no explanation, no markdown outside the JSON.
